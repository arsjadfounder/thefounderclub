At The Founder Club, we recognize that privacy and data protection are paramount to our esteemed members. Data Collection & Processing

What Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when voluntarily provided through our secure digital gateway, including:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address
  • Professional and business details
  • Any additional information you choose to share

Secure Entry Processing via Formspree

Our secure digital gateway processes all member entries through Formspree, a trusted data processing platform. All data transmission is encrypted and protected under strict non-disclosure guidelines and binding confidentiality agreements. Formspree operates with enterprise-grade security measures, ensuring that your information is never exposed to unauthorized third parties.

We do not retain data longer than necessary for the stated purposes. Once your entry has been processed and your membership status determined, data is either securely archived or permanently deleted in accordance with GDPR requirements and your expressed preferences.

GDPR Compliance & Your Rights

The Founder Club fully complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and recognizes your fundamental rights as a data subject:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request and receive a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request limitations on how we use your information.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to Object: You can object to certain processing activities.
  • Rights Related to Automated Decision Making: You have protections regarding decisions made solely by automated means.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section below.

Absolute Data Confidentiality

Your data confidentiality is absolute and inviolable. We maintain the following standards:

  • Zero Unauthorized Disclosure: We never share, sell, or disclose your personal information to external parties without explicit consent and legal obligation.
  • Encrypted Storage: All data is stored in encrypted databases with restricted access limited to authorized personnel only.
  • Non-Disclosure Agreements: All employees and partners handling your data sign strict non-disclosure agreements binding them to confidentiality.
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct regular security assessments and penetration testing to identify and remediate vulnerabilities.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls ensure only necessary personnel can view specific data elements.
  • Audit Logging: All access to personal data is logged and monitored for suspicious activity.

Third-Party Data Processors

We work exclusively with trusted data processors who meet our rigorous security and confidentiality standards. Our only primary data processor is Formspree, which operates under:

  • GDPR-compliant data processing agreements
  • Enterprise-level encryption standards
  • Strict data retention and deletion policies
  • Regular independent security audits
  • Binding confidentiality obligations and non-disclosure guidelines

We do not engage any secondary processors without explicit member notification and consent.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Active Members: Data is retained for the duration of your membership and relationship with The Founder Club.
  • Membership Inquiries: Inquiry data is retained for up to 12 months unless you request deletion sooner.
  • Legal & Compliance: Data may be retained longer if legally required or necessary for legal proceedings.
  • Deletion Upon Request: You may request permanent deletion at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your data:

  • Secure authentication protocols and multi-factor authorization
